# BAU Operating System - Bootstrap Guide

Welcome to the **Anticariat Albert BAU Workspace**.

This repository operates the day-to-day business after the Shopify migration:

- `integrations/`: Connectors for Shopify webhooks, Romanian courier APIs, and invoicing.
- `marketing/`: Customer segmentation engine and personalized campaign generators.
- `runbooks/`: Operational procedures for shop maintenance and customer support.
