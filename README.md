# Anticariat Albert - Business As Usual (BAU) Operating System

## Purpose

This repository houses the long-lived, post-migration production software, business automations, customer segmentation pipelines, and executive interfaces for **Anticariat Albert**.

## Core Philosophy

1. **Operator Friction Minimum**: Marius's mother is the primary user. She must receive clear, actionable digests (orders to pack, sales pulse, one-click approvals) without technical complexity.
2. **Stable Orchestration**: Moving away from unstable beta harnesses toward reliable headless tools (Hermes / OpenClaw / DeepSeek Harness or deterministic Python workers).
3. **Hyper-Personalized Customer Relationship Management**:
   - Leveraging 15 years of customer purchase history.
   - Targeting specific customer segments (e.g. art history collectors, philosophy readers, medical professionals) with curated book arrivals.
